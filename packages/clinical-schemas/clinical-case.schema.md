# Clinical Case Schema

**Schema Name:** Clinical Case  
**Schema ID:** PT-CS-001  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Official Platform Contract  
**Owner:** PharmaTrybe Architecture Team

---

# Purpose

The Clinical Case Schema represents the canonical patient case exchanged throughout the PharmaTrybe platform.

Every clinical assessment begins with a Clinical Case.

This schema serves as the **single source of truth** for all downstream services.

It is consumed by:

- SOAR/GSK AI Engine
- ARMD AI Engine
- WHO Knowledge Engine
- Clinical Decision Engine
- Explainability Engine

No downstream service should require the frontend to construct a different payload.

---

# Design Principles

The Clinical Case is designed to be:

- Clinically meaningful
- Explainable
- Auditable
- Extensible
- Vendor independent
- AI independent

The schema intentionally avoids implementation-specific details.

|--------|------|----------|-------------|

# Clinical Presentation

Represents why the patient is being assessed.

| Field | Type | Required |
|--------|------|----------|
| syndrome | Enum | Yes |
| severity | Enum | Yes |
| acquisition | Enum | Yes |
| symptoms | Array<String> | Optional |
| duration_days | Integer | Optional |

Example syndromes include:

- Community Acquired Pneumonia
- Hospital Acquired Pneumonia
- Ventilator Associated Pneumonia
- COPD Exacerbation
- Bronchiectasis
- Influenza Secondary Infection

Future syndromes can be added without breaking compatibility.

# Validation Rules

Age

- must be ≥0
- must be ≤120

Weight

- must be >0

Severity

Allowed values:

- Mild
- Moderate
- Severe

Acquisition

Allowed values:

- Community
- Hospital
- ICU

# Relationship to Other Schemas

This schema is consumed by:

- Respiratory Schema (SOAR)
- ARMD Schema
- WHO Schema

These services **must never modify** the Clinical Case.

Instead, they produce their own response schemas.

---

# Backward Compatibility

Future additions must be optional.

Existing required fields cannot be removed.

Breaking changes require Schema Version 2.0.

---

# Governance

The Clinical Case Schema is the master clinical contract for PharmaTrybe.

All frontend forms, backend APIs, AI engines, and clinical decision workflows shall conform to this schema.

No service may redefine patient case structures independently.
