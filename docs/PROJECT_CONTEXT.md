# PharmaTrybe — Project Context

## Project Vision

PharmaTrybe is an Explainable Artificial Intelligence Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) designed to support evidence-based antimicrobial prescribing and antimicrobial stewardship.

The platform is intended to support clinicians—not replace clinical judgement.

Every recommendation produced by PharmaTrybe must be transparent, explainable, reproducible, auditable, and aligned with evidence-based medicine.

---

# Core Design Principles

1. Clinical evidence always comes before AI predictions.

2. Explainability is mandatory.

3. Patient safety has higher priority than predictive performance.

4. Recommendations must always remain clinician-assisted rather than autonomous.

5. Medical knowledge must remain independent from AI models.

6. Every recommendation must be traceable to supporting evidence.

---

# Platform Architecture

PharmaTrybe consists of five major intelligent services.

## 1. SOAR/GSK AI Service

Primary AI model.

Purpose:

- Respiratory antimicrobial stewardship.
- Syndrome-specific recommendations.
- Respiratory infection prediction.
- Clinical risk assessment.

SOAR/GSK is responsible for respiratory stewardship intelligence only.

It does NOT make final antibiotic decisions.

---

## 2. ARMD AI Service

Secondary AI model.

Purpose:

Predict antimicrobial resistance using hospital microbiology datasets.

Current development status:

WP2 Feature Engineering.

Future inputs include:

- Prior antibiotic exposure
- Previous infecting organisms
- ICU exposure
- Hospital ward
- Laboratory biomarkers
- Vital signs
- Procedures
- Demographics
- Previous resistance history

ARMD predicts resistance probability.

ARMD never selects antibiotics.

---

## 3. WHO Knowledge Service

Shared knowledge engine.

Contains:

- WHO AWaRe classification
- WHO antimicrobial guidance
- Stewardship recommendations
- Future WHO clinical knowledge

This service is shared by every AI model.

It contains no machine learning.

---

## 4. Clinical Decision Engine

This is the brain of PharmaTrybe.

Responsibilities:

- Combine SOAR/GSK outputs
- Combine ARMD predictions
- Query WHO knowledge
- Apply stewardship rules
- Generate evidence-supported recommendations

This is the only service allowed to generate clinical recommendations.

---

## 5. Explainability Engine

Responsible for:

- Explaining every recommendation
- Displaying contributing evidence
- Showing AI confidence
- Showing WHO evidence
- Showing decision pathway
- Producing clinician-friendly explanations

No recommendation is complete without explainability.

---

# Clinical Philosophy

The platform does NOT claim:

"AI chooses the best antibiotic."

Instead the platform provides:

"Evidence-supported antimicrobial options generated by combining AI predictions with WHO guideline knowledge."

---

# Two Clinical Scenarios

## Scenario 1

Known or predicted antimicrobial resistance.

Workflow:

SOAR/GSK
↓

ARMD predicts resistance

↓

WHO Knowledge

↓

Clinical Decision Engine

↓

Alternative evidence-supported antibiotics

---

## Scenario 2

No predicted resistance.

Workflow:

SOAR/GSK

↓

WHO Knowledge

↓

Clinical Decision Engine

↓

# Engineering Principles

This repository follows an API-first architecture.

Every service is independently deployable.

Every service owns its own responsibility.

Services communicate only through well-defined contracts.

No service may directly access another service's internal implementation.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Build an enterprise-grade Explainable AI antimicrobial stewardship platform suitable for research, hospitals, and future clinical deployment.
