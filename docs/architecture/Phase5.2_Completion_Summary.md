## Architecture Compliance

### Core Principles (From PharmaTrybe Constitution)

✅ Evidence precedes artificial intelligence
- WHO plugin returns evidence only, not predictions

✅ Artificial intelligence supports clinicians
- Plugin provides clinical knowledge for clinician review

✅ AI never replaces clinicians
- Plugin is decision support, not autonomous

✅ WHO knowledge is authoritative clinical source
- Plugin uses WHO database as authoritative source

✅ SOAR/GSK predicts resistance evidence only
- WHO plugin integrates with SOAR/GSK predictions

✅ ARMD predicts hospital resistance risk only
- WHO plugin integrates with ARMD risk assessment

✅ Only Clinical Decision Engine synthesizes recommendations
- WHO plugin doesn't synthesize, only provides evidence

✅ Explainability Engine explains every recommendation
- WHO evidence available to explainability

✅ Knowledge and prediction remain independent
- WHO plugin executes independently of predictions

✅ Every recommendation must be auditable
- WHO evidence traceable with citation and source

✅ Every component independently deployable
- WHO plugin can deploy independently

✅ Clinical safety has priority over predictive performance
- Error isolation ensures graceful degradation
